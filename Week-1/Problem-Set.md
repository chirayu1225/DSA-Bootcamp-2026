## How to Approach These Problems

1. Open the link and read the problem statement carefully.
2. Click "Try It Yourself" - solve on your own first.
3. Spend at least **20–25 minutes** thinking and trying to optimize before looking at solutions.
4. After attempting, read through the better and optimal approaches in the article.
5. Even if your solution works, try to reduce time complexity, reduce space complexity, improve readability, and write cleaner logic.
6. Dry run your code on the provided test cases.
7. Test edge cases: empty arrays, single-element arrays, negative numbers, large inputs.
8. Focus on **why** the approach works, not on memorizing the code.
9. After every solution, write down the time and space complexity.
10. If stuck, do not immediately search for code. Try drawing examples, observing patterns, simplifying the problem, or solving smaller cases manually.
